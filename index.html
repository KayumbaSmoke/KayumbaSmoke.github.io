ABOUT SMOKE

Professionally known as Kayumba Nathan Simunhwenze, Smoke is a Zambian comedian, actor and writer building connections with audiences through live comedy.

Based in Lusaka, he has performed in Zambia, South Africa, Eswatini, Botswana and Zimbabwe.
