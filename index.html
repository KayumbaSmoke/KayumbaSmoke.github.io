ABOUT SMOKE

Professionally known as Kayumba Nathan Simunhwenze, Smoke is a Zambian stand-up comedian, actor and writer based in Lusaka, Zambia.

His performance experience spans Zambia, South Africa, Eswatini, Botswana and Zimbabwe. He is building connections with audiences, fellow comedians, event organisers and festival promoters across the continent and beyond.

Whether it's a comedy showcase, festival, corporate event or hosted function, Smoke is available to discuss opportunities and collaborations.
